## Question

Two questions, one shared mechanism (self-supervised predictive error as an
unbounded learning signal):

1. **Ladder (E1).** Does within-lifetime competence on the hard 150-target
   passage keep climbing with the *dose of self-supervised signal* (teacher-
   forced passes), where scalar-reward learning demonstrably saturates?
2. **Acquisition (E2).** In `generalize` mode — every instance a freshly
   generated snippet, sealed text never seen during evolution — can evolution +
   lifetime predictive coding produce a brain that acquires arbitrary novel
   text within its own lifetime, above chance?

## Hypothesis

Predictive coding's error signal is generated by the learner against the
environment stream itself, so unlike a fixed reward landscape it has no
saturation point set by the task's payoff structure.

Falsifiers: E1 — passes 64 lands at or below passes 32; E2 — sealed accuracy
stays near chance.

## Result

### E1 — ladder dose-response on the hard passage

| Passes | Sealed /150 | Notes |
|---:|---:|---|
| 32 (anchor) | **95** (63.3%) | dev-audit monotone 50.7→63.3%, still rising at g149 |
| 64 | **101** (67.3%) | dev-audit monotone 52.7→67.3%, still rising at g149 |

**The archived 07-20 anchors do not replicate**: the entire current codebase is
one commit (`befdf23`, WANN-NEAT cutover included) that postdates the PC sweep;
101@16p / 115@32p belong to a superseded substrate revision. All cross-revision
comparisons are void; this experiment's internal comparison stands. The BRIEF's
"best plausible = 115/150" must be read as 95–101/150 on the current revision.

Relative dose rule: +6 ≥ +5 over the fresh anchor → **ladder supported
internally**; doubling lifetime data still buys real tokens where scalar reward
was flat by g1000. Both champions are again **0% recurrent** (894/892 edges,
52 hidden nodes): self-supervised categorical output error still does not
recruit recurrence at this budget.

### E2 — open-ended acquisition (never-seen generated snippets)

| Arm | Sealed acc (12 snippets × 33 chars) | Selected gen | Champion |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| seed 7 | **73.48%** (291/396) | 124 | 39 hidden / 876 edges / 0 recurrent |
| seed 27 | **63.13%** (250/396) | 174 | 59 hidden / 906 edges / 0 recurrent |
| seed 7, relays OFF | **68.43%** (271/396) | 149 | 39 hidden / 872 edges / 0 recurrent |

Chance = 3.57%. Acquisition is robust across seeds and decisively passes the
≥20% success gate. Exact-sequence rate 0/12 everywhere. Delay-relay ablation:
−5.1 points absolute (−7% relative, inside seed noise ~±5) → the temporal-relay
operator is **not load-bearing** for acquisition.

Topology grows steadily in every arm (1 → 40–59 hidden nodes, 813 → ~900 edges)
and continues growing after accuracy plateaus (~gen 100) — structural
complexification decouples from competence gains under weak minimality pressure
(likely partly neutral drift; no complexity objective exists).

### Frozen-champion causal diagnostics (seed-7 champion, 12 unseen snippets)

Lifetime pass dose (length 32):

| passes | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 (evolution budget) | 16 | 32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| sealed acc | 17.2% | 30.6% | 55.3% | 72.0% | 77.3% | 78.0% |

- **Lifetime learning is unambiguously causal**: the inherited-prior floor is
  only ~17%; scaling lifetime data 32× lifts sealed accuracy to 78%.
- The curve bends near ~78% — per-snippet saturation below exact mastery,
  consistent with 0/12 exact trials.
- Length transfer at fixed 16 passes: 77.3% (len 32) → 64.8% (48) → 56.5% (64):
  graceful degradation, capacity-limited.

### Follow-up arms same day (matched to fresh anchors)

| Arm | Sealed /150 | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| 96p, g150 | **105** (70.0%) | dev-audit still rising at g149; 50 hidden nodes, 0 recurrent |
| 32p, g300 | **102** (68.0%) | audit decelerating but unplateaued through g299; 63 hidden nodes, 0 recurrent |

Hard-passage internal response surface (p512/s101, working-tree state):

- Passes axis: 95 → 101 → 105 across 32/64/96 passes — **perfectly linear
  (+5 per +32 passes), zero saturation** in range.
- Depth axis: 95 → 102 from g150→g300 — monotone but decelerating.
- Dose is both more effective and more compute-efficient than depth
  (~1 token/93 s vs ~1 token/106 s of wall time).
- Linear extrapolation puts the 135/150 gate near ~288 passes (~24 min/run) if
  linearity holds; confirm at 128p before committing to that budget.
- Every champion on this task remains pure feedforward plastic readout.

## Decision rule outcomes

- E1: relative rule met (+6 over fresh anchor) → extend dose next iteration.
  Preregistered absolute bands voided by anchor drift, disclosed above.
- E2: ≥20% gate passed on both seeds → scale snippet length next iteration.
- Relay-off drop < 30% relative → operator retained as neutral option, not
  credited as the acquisition mechanism.

## Interpretation and next decision

Progress against the three-pillar goal:

1. **Environment complexification works now.** `generalize` mode converts
   next-token from a fixed-panel benchmark into an infinite novel-text
   distribution with continuously scalable difficulty (`--snippet-length`),
   and selection already produces brains that acquire unseen text in one
   lifetime (63–73%). This is the first clean instantiation of open-ended
   environment pressure in the current substrate — and it needs zero new code.
2. **Genome complexification is visible but uncoupled from competence.**
   Structure grows steadily under PC selection but winners remain pure
   feedforward plastic-readout learners; recurrence, relays, and temporal codes
   are all bypassed. Exact-sequence 0/12 says acquisition *never completes*
   within the plasticity horizon — the binding constraint for deeper cognition.
3. **The ladder is real but architecture-bounded.** Self-supervised signal
   scales competence monotonically (hard-passage dose response; frozen-champion
   17→78%) yet bends at ~78% without forming temporal state.

Next iteration, in priority order:

- **E-next-1:** hard-passage dose extension to passes 96/128 (toward the BRIEF
  gate of 135/150); depth arm g300@32p since both audit curves were still
  climbing at g149.
- **E-next-2:** acquisition scaling curve — snippet length 48/64/96 with
  proportionally more passes and instances; find where acquisition breaks and
  whether topology growth re-couples to competence there.
- **E-next-3 (the frontier):** close-the-string experiments. Exact-trial rate 0
  means the last ~22% resists pure readout memorization. Test levers that could
  let lifetime learning recruit temporal state: nlms normalization, evolved
  initial_learning_rate headroom, and the known-open within-lifetime recurrent
  learning rule. A genome that finishes novel strings exactly would be the first
  evidence of learned temporal memory under the architecture bar.
